Our Story

Founded on the principle of Ataraxy—a state of serene calmness and freedom from emotional disturbance—our spa was created as a sanctuary from the relentless pace of modern life.

"We believe that true wellness begins the moment you decide to pause."

At Ataraxy Wellness Spa, we don't just offer treatments; we offer rituals. Our team is dedicated to restoring your natural balance through a blend of traditional Filipino healing techniques and modern therapeutic practices.

Whether you visit us at our serene Metro Manila location or invite us into your home through our mobile service, our goal remains the same: to help you find your center and leave feeling renewed.
